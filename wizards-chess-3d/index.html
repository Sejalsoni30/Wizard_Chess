The Sacred Code of the Cathedral Altar

Wizard's Chess follows classical tournament chess rules with arcane extensions:

  • Check & Checkmate: When a King is in Check, it pulses with a crimson heartbeat aura and thunder flashes through the stained-glass Rose window.
  • Blunder Premonition: Pieces tremble nervously with high-frequency micro-jitters when placed into danger or about to be sacrificed.
  • Battlefield Scars: Permanent floor cracks, scorch marks, and 3D rubble stay on squares where pieces died.
  • Physical Sand Timers: In Blitz (3m) and Rapid (10m) modes, physical 3D crystal hourglasses drain sand continuously on the altar.
