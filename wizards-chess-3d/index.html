Wizard's Chess 3D Architectural Blueprint

1. Decoupled EventBus Architecture

Strict publish/subscribe model decoupling the Three.js 3D Viewport, chess.js Logic, Minimax AI Worker, GSAP Animation Choreographer, Web Audio Engine, and DOM-based MD3 HUD.

2. Zero External Assets (100% Procedural)

All piece silhouettes (Pawns, Rooks, Knights, Bishops, Queens, Kings) are synthesized via compound Three.js LatheGeometry, ExtrudeGeometry, and CSG-like primitives. Materials use canvas-generated procedural noise bump maps and crack veins for Carrara White Marble and Volcanic Obsidian.

3. 4-State Animation Pipeline & Combat Choreography

  • IDLE: Dynamic vertical sine-wave levitation with phase offsets and counter-rotating glowing base rune rings.
  • MOVE: Dual-axis GSAP parabolic curve elevation with velocity-aligned tilt angle and procedural stone sliding acoustic rumble.
  • ATTACK: Attacker coordinates glide toward victim, halts at striking threshold, charges arcane energy, and delivers a dynamic lunge strike.
  • SHATTER: At the instant of impact, victim geometry fractures into 30 polyhedral stone shards with radial outward impulse, -18 m/s² gravity, floor bounce restitution, shockwave ripple, and a 4-second dissolve cycle.

4. Web Audio Synthesizer Engine

Custom Web Audio graph with bandpass noise generators, sub-bass oscillators, harmonic bell resonators, and frequency sweeps—generating stone slides, kinetic impacts, crystalline ruptures, and checkmate chords without external audio files.

5. Minimax AI with Alpha-Beta Pruning

Heuristic evaluation integrating piece material values and positional piece-square tables (PST) for opening, midgame, and endgame pressure.
